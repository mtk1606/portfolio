# Newspaper press photographs

The three photographs supplied by the portfolio owner were adapted with image generation into warm monochrome press illustrations, preserving the recognizable buildings and signage. Prompt direction: fine halftone, soot-black ink, ivory paper, restrained ink spread and fiber; no added headlines, buildings, people or fantasy objects.

Assets: `public/assets/press-bmo-photo-v1.webp`, `press-ntt-photo-v1.webp`, and `press-courtyard-photo-v1.webp`. BMO and NTT accompany their matching editions. The courtyard is explicitly atmospheric and does not represent an AstraSage workplace. The large inspection view follows the current edition. CSS adds a narrow inset plate, faint print lines and paper blending.
