# Object arrivals

Radio settles with a brief dial/needle tune and warm light; notebook opens a charcoal cover and turns three paper leaves; newspaper relaxes a shallow fold; arcade screen and marquee warm up without flicker. Effects begin only after portfolio entry and either 18% intersection or a viewport-relative visible strip, play once per session, and never lock scrolling or hide native content. Pointer/focus interaction, leaving the viewport, a hidden tab, and reduced-motion changes cancel and clean up immediately. The reading spread and newspaper text remain stationary; only decorative paper layers move. The marquee begins before the screen wakes. Storage restrictions fall back to in-memory once-only tracking. Narrow screens omit the decorative page overlays. Scroll arrivals make no sound. Existing controls, data, audio and black-hole renderer remain unchanged.

Validated with object-arrival lifecycle tests and production build.
