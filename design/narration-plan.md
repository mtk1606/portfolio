# Optional narration contract

First visit: Show me around / Explore freely. Text guidance is available today; voice is not enabled or generated. Return visits enter quietly. The Guide button resumes context without forcing a replay.

When recorded narration is added: start only with explicit voice opt-in; keep captions independently available; one clip at a time; pause/close/navigation cancels speech and restores ambience; duck ambience while speaking; never speak on hover; keep text, keyboard and direct project routes usable without voice. Respect reduced motion independently from audio.

Draft opening: “Welcome. This is a small collection of systems I’ve built and questions I’ve worked through. You can explore freely, or I can show you a few things.”
ASTER: “This machine explains one small part of ASTER: earlier orders at the same price go first. The actual project reconstructs a recorded Nasdaq order book. Open the engineering record to see the choices, checks, and limitations.”
Experience: “The newspaper contains my professional experience. The role and scope come first; the optional design stories are fictional.”
Closing: “That’s the short tour. You can keep exploring, read my résumé, or get in touch. The work and source links are always available.”
