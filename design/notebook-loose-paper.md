# Loose research paper

A photographic blank material replaces the flat discovery-sheet fill. Native charts, source links, tabs and slider remain unchanged. The material adds worn cellulose, faded notebook ruling, small edge nicks and corner creases. CSS supplies a restrained irregular silhouette, slight rotation and contact shadow.

Mynerve (SIL OFL, bundled beside its license) is used for handwritten prompts and headings. Dense evidence, axis labels and numerical source material retain their readable existing type.

Asset: public/assets/research-loose-paper-v1.webp. Generated with the built-in image tool. Prompt: perfectly overhead old loose notebook paper, muted oatmeal ivory, fine cellulose fibers, faint blue-gray ruling and red margin, handled edges and small tears, subtle corner crease and tea mark at outer margin, clean center; no text, objects, charts or dark stains.
