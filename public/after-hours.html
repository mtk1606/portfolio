      Flight manual / pauses flight +

      Thrust pushes in the direction your nose points. Tilt to travel sideways, then tilt the other way to brake. Level the feet before contact. Short, early burns make a softer landing than one late rescue.

      Land entirely inside the lit pad: vertical speed 0–30, horizontal speed −22 to 22, and tilt within 9°. Fuel and a softer touchdown earn bonus points. Each landing advances to a tighter site; a crash costs one ship.
