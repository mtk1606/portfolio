Word validation: ENABLE lexicon by M. Cooper and Alan Beale, public-domain word list, mirrored at https://github.com/dolph/dictionary/blob/master/enable1.txt . Filtered here to alphabetic words of 2–15 letters.
Five-letter answers: common-word subset from https://github.com/dolph/dictionary/blob/master/popular.txt , intersected with ENABLE and filtered for length.
